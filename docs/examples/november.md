# November: fun flying

**Footage:** 5 GoPro clips (3 at 25 fps H.264, 2 at 50 fps HEVC): a snowy field and a red house with a porch.
2 DJI goggle recordings without gyro were left out.
**Task:** "весёлый ролик с крутыми полётами" (a fun clip with cool flying), about a minute.

**How:** 4-beat shots in loud phrases (never shorter), 1.1-1.2x there, up to two hero ramps of different
kinds, `W_SYNC` 3, frame blending for slow motion of 25 fps sources. Three ~1 min variants: Rhinoceros
(126 BPM), Who Likes to Party (117), Blue Ska (110). "Rocket Power" was dropped: no constant grid. These
options later became `--lively`, `--heroes`, `--pace`.
