# Two months at the dacha

**Footage:** 26 DJI O3 Air Unit clips (4K 16:9) and 2 GoPro HERO9 clips from May and June, nothing
stabilized. The named mp4s in the folder were the owner's old exports of the same originals.
**Task:** "two months at the dacha": calm (country) music, long shots, the cool flights first, several
variants; stabilize the chosen pieces with Gyroflow.

**How:** every original stabilized whole at 640×360 (~85 fps, 30 min for 52 min of footage) as the proxy;
GoPro motion through Gyroflow's export; chapters by recording day in date order, scenes in proportion to each
day's good flying (a day with nothing usable passes its scene on); only 8- and 16-beat shots at 1x, no ramps,
no heroes; piloting first (`W_SKILL` 4, `W_SYNC` 1.5). The 1080p finals: Gyroflow pieces per shot, a 0.5 s
dissolve between days and a date caption ("23 мая") at the start of each day.

The O3 sees its red propellers in the wide view, and stabilization moves them up the frame: on one day on
35-67 % of frames, later 2-19 %. A props detector (saturated red in the bottom corners) excluded those frames
and the final cut off the bottom 16 %. The owner later ruled that propellers are not the edit's problem: after
stabilization they must not be in view, and if they are, that is Gyroflow's zoom.
